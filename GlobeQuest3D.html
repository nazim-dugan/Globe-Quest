Globe Quest

Spin the globe and click the country you're asked to find. Ten rounds: 100 points for a first-try hit, less for each miss, and the answer is revealed after three misses. Each wrong guess shows a closeness score from 1 (bordering) to 10 (far away), and the country is tinted from hot red to cold blue.

Not sure? You have 3 flag challenges: pick the right flag out of four and the country counts as found. It scores 100 points if you use it before any guess, 60 after one guess and 30 after two. Pick wrong and you lose the round.

Two ways to play. Find: you are given a name and click the country on the globe. Name: a country is highlighted and you pick its name from six options. Scoring and flag challenges work the same in both.
